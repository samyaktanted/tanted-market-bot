🏢 REITs & InvITs, explained

Want a slice of real estate or infrastructure without buying a whole building or highway? That's what these trusts let you do:

🏬 REIT — owns rent-earning property (malls, offices, warehouses)
🛣️ InvIT — owns income infrastructure (roads, power lines, pipelines)

Both list on the exchange, you buy 'units', and they pass most of their income back to you as regular distributions.

Remember: unit prices swing with the market and are sensitive to interest rates. Save this 🔖 and follow for more.
