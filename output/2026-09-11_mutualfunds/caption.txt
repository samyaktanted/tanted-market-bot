💰 Mutual Funds 101 — the 2-minute beginner's guide

A mutual fund pools money from many investors and a professional manager invests it in a basket of stocks and/or bonds — giving you instant diversification, even with a small SIP.

Types: 📈 Equity (growth) • 🏦 Debt (stability) • ⚖️ Hybrid (both) • 📊 Index/ETF (low-cost)

Key terms: NAV, expense ratio, AUM, exit load.
