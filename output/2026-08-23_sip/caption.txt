📈 The power of SIP

A Systematic Investment Plan invests a fixed amount automatically every month — so you build wealth on autopilot without trying to time the market.

Why it works: rupee-cost averaging (buy more when cheap) + compounding (returns earning returns).

How to choose a fund (we don't recommend specific schemes): match it to your goal, prefer a low expense ratio, and check long-term consistency — not last year's chart.

Save this 🔖 and start small. Even ₹500/month adds up.
